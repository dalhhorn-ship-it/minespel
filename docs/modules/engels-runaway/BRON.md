## Phrases

- Excuse me. = Sorry.
- When does the train to Oxford leave? = Wanneer vertrekt de trein naar Oxford?
- The train leaves at twelve o’clock. = De trein vertrekt om twaalf uur.
- From which platform? = Van welk perron?
- It leaves from platform 10. = Hij vertrekt van perron 10.
- Thank you. = Dankjewel.
- You’re welcome. = Graag gedaan.

## Zo zeg je hoe laat het is

- 11:00 = eleven o’clock
- 11:30 = half past eleven
- 11:15 = a quarter past eleven
- 11:45 = a quarter to twelve

The sheet uses the curly apostrophe (’) in o’clock and You’re.
