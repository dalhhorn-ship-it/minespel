# Changelog

Written so kids can read it too.

## Unreleased

## 0.16.0

### Nog een keer

* Did you get a question wrong twice? Then you see the answer, and at the end of the pauze it comes back so you can try it again.
* The same happens in Extra oefenen. And when a villager asks you something and you get it wrong twice, the villager asks it once more.
* You get one try each time. Right? You see a green check. Wrong? You see the answer again and it comes back a bit later. Every question comes back at most 3 times.
* It costs no points. Not one. Not even on Moeilijk.
* "Nog 2 te gaan" shows how many are left. On the summary you can read "En 1 alsnog goed!".
* With a villager, "Laat maar" is always there, and a repeat never gives a block.
* For papa or mama: exams stay as they were. There is no new setting. The repeat answers are saved on the iPad in the answer history and in the backup, and they change no numbers in Voortgang.
## 0.15.0

### Geluid dichtbij

* You can now hear the black bubble, Klodder, the Monstermol and your Lavakip-bar. Each one has its own soft, silly sound.
* The closer you come, the louder it gets. Far away you hear nothing. Turn around and the sound moves to the left or the right ear.
* At most 2 of these sounds play at the same time, so it never gets noisy.
* With "Geluidseffecten" off or the volume at 0 you hear nothing, as before. In the learning break, an exam and the shop it is quiet too.
* For papa or mama: nothing new is saved and nothing goes over the internet.

## 0.14.0

### Lavakip-bar

* In the Dinowinkel you can buy a food truck for 60 dino points. It stands near your home.
* Order a lavakip for 3 points. You can carry at most 10.
* Hold one and tap a dino, a golden dino, a chicken or a knight, then "Lavakip geven". It munches for 3 seconds, does a silly dance and says "Hap! Lekker pittig!". A few hearts and a small orange puff show up. It is a snack, not fire.
* With "less motion" on, there is no dance and no moving puff. The words still show.
* Feeding changes nothing else: the animal keeps its name, its mode and its spot.
* For papa or mama: the backup holds the Lavakip-bar. A backup from 0.13.0 still works. 0.13.0 cannot read a backup from 0.14.0 and says so in a friendly way.

## 0.13.0

### Duidelijke mol en kompas

* The Monstermol now says when he comes: the card and the lines tell you. When he is up, a line says "Bouw een hek van 2 hoog om hem heen."
* He stays until you build a pen 2 blocks high around him, up to 6 x 6. Then he is caught, calm and asleep. You can keep up to 3 caught moles and 1 free one.
* A knight can chase him away for 60 to 90 seconds. Then he sneaks back. A knight guards at most 4 animals. A knight is never needed: a pen always works.
* A knight has a fourth mode: "Jaag monsters weg".
* The mole teases an animal at most once every 15 seconds. No animal is ever lost.
* There is a new round lilac bubble under the learning break pill. It counts down to the next black bubble and points at the bubble, Klodder or the free mole. It does nothing when you tap it.
* For papa or mama: the backup holds the moles now (backup format 6). A backup from 0.12.0 still works. 0.12.0 cannot read a backup from 0.13.0 and says so in a friendly way.

## 0.12.0

### Meer uitrusting

* There is much more gear for your knights: 13 new things. You can now dress a knight in 8 places: head, shield, hand, back, body, feet, flag and mount.
* New armour: a Maliënkolder, a Glanzend harnas that shines, a Koningscape and a Drakenschild. New helmets: a Pluimhelm and a Gouden helm that shines. New boots: Snelle laarzen.
* A Houten zwaard, a Banier and a Koningsbanier. A knight with a banner sees monsters and animals from further away.
* A Strijdpaard, a war horse, and a Rij-triceratops, a triceratops to ride on. Your knight sits on top and walks faster.
* The Bubbelstaf blows soap bubbles while your knight pokes. They pop away by themselves. With "less motion" on, there are no bubbles.
* In the Dinowinkel the row "Uitrusting" now has 21 things. A knight can still do everything without gear. Gear only makes it a little faster, never too fast.
* For papa or mama: the backup holds the new gear. A backup from 0.11.0 still works. 0.11.0 cannot read a backup from 0.12.0 and says so in a friendly way.

## 0.11.0

### Diepe edelstenen

* Deep under the striped layer there are 8 shiny stones to find: Maansteen, Zonnesteen, Jade, Opaal, Topaas, Parelsteen, Drakensteen and Mollengoud. Mine one and you find it. A message tells you its name.
* In the Dinowinkel there is a new row "Edelstenen". Once you have found a stone, you can buy it with dino points. You buy each stone once.
* A stone you bought is yours. Build with it as much as you like from the Diep tab, it costs nothing more. Mining a stone block you built never loses the stone.
* If you start a new world, you keep the stones you found and bought, and all stones are in the ground again.
* For papa or mama: the backup now holds the stones. A backup from 0.10.0 or older still works, with no stones. 0.10.0 cannot read a backup from 0.11.0 and says so in a friendly way.

## 0.10.0

### Uitrusting voor je ridders

* In the Dinowinkel there is a new row "Uitrusting". You can buy 8 things for your knights with your dino points: a Leren kap, a Houten schild, a Riddercape, a Kietelveer, an IJzeren helm, an IJzeren schild, a Glimzwaard and a Pony. You buy each one once.
* Tap a knight and then "Uitrusten". You see your knight in a picture. Tap a thing to put it on. Tap it again to take it off. Things you do not use wait in your wapenkast, and you can move them to another knight.
* A knight with gear sends monsters off faster. The Pony makes him walk faster too. A knight without gear can still do it, only a little slower.
* You see the gear on your knight in the world.
* Nothing you bought is ever lost. If you trade in a knight, his gear goes back in the wapenkast. If you start a new world, you keep your gear.
* The Kietelveer tickles with a soft "hihihi".
* For papa or mama: the backup now holds the gear. A backup from 0.9.0 still works. 0.9.0 cannot read a backup from 0.10.0 and says so in a friendly way.

## 0.9.0

### Monstermol

* If you dig past the striped layer, a silly Monstermol wakes up. First the ground rumbles for a moment. Then he comes up out of the grass, a little way from you, near your animals.
* He is purple, with big glasses and a pink nose. He walks to an animal that walks around free, says "Boe!" and teases it. The animal jumps and runs a few steps. He never takes a block and never gives or takes points.
* Two warnings come first. Dig deep and a message says someone lives down there. Mine a striped warning block and a card says "Niet verder graven!". You have to tap OK.
* A wall 2 blocks high round your animals stops him. He says "Hmpf! Ik kan er niet bij." and goes back into the ground. If nobody is there to play with, or after 10 minutes, he goes to sleep.
* Your knights chase him away too. When enough knights poke him, he says "Oké, oké, ik ga al!" and leaves. The knights also find their way round walls now, and Klodder too.
* Everything stands still when you are in a break, the shop, a menu or the parent area. Nothing of the mole is saved: after you close the game he is gone.
* For papa or mama: a row "Monstermol" in the card "Monsters en ridders" in Instellingen. It is on for everybody. Switch it off and he never comes.
* Tapping the black bubble now works on any part of it, also the left part. The first tap after the game starts is no longer read as a drag.
* For the builders: the test of the Dinofluit waited on a moment that did not always come. It now waits for a real condition.

## 0.8.0

### Grote wereld

* The world is now much bigger: 128 x 128 blocks and 80 high. You can walk far in every direction, and an invisible wall keeps you inside at the edge.
* You can dig much deeper now, about 20 blocks down. Everything you built is still there in the middle of the big world, with new land with grass and trees all around it.
* Deep down there is a striped layer. Under it you find hidden shiny blocks. At the very bottom the ground is too hard to dig.
* Everything you own moves along: your spot, your animals, your knights and your villages. The move happens once when the game starts. If the iPad switches off halfway, the old save stays whole and the move runs again. An old backup still works too.
* The game only draws what the camera can see, and the view ends where the fog ends. `?near=1` in the address is a spare switch that builds only the chunks near you.
* New for papa or mama: a row "Kijkafstand" in Instellingen, with Ver (as before) and Dichtbij. Dichtbij makes the game faster on an older iPad, because you then look less far. It is saved with the settings and in the backup.
* For the builders: two flaky browser tests are now steady (they waited on a clock instead of on what they needed).

## 0.7.0

### Ridders

* In the Dinowinkel you can now buy a knight for 30 points (at most 3). He guards your animals. Trade him in for 15 points if you like.
* Tap a knight and a menu opens: Volg mij, Blijf hier or Pas op de dieren. A guard knight tells you how many dinos and chickens he watches. He is a friendly cartoon knight with a helmet, a tabard and a wooden sword, and a soft fanfare.
* Your knights are saved with your world and come back after a backup restore.
* Papa or mama get a card "Monsters en ridders" in Instellingen to switch knights on or off.

### Zwarte bubbel

* After a while of playing, a little monster in a black bubble may come floating by. His name is Klodder. Tap the bubble and he jumps out, giggling.
* Klodder is a bit silly. He nibbles a few blocks and teases your dinos and chickens, who jump and run away a bit, then come back. Nobody gets hurt.
* Your knight sends him off. Watch the little bar over Klodder fill up: more knights make it go faster. Then Klodder floats away in his bubble and says "Doei!". If nobody sends him off, he gets tired by himself after 5 minutes.
* Every block he nibbled comes back, always, and your own building is never touched. Nothing he does is ever saved.
* Papa or mama can switch the Zwarte bubbel in Instellingen, on the same card. It only works when Ridders is on. For a game that was there before, it starts switched off.

## 0.6.0 (2026-11-15)

### Backup

* Papa or mama can now keep your world and dinos safe in a backup. In the parent area, open Import & Export and tap Backup maken. The file keeps everything except the pincode. Tap Backup terugzetten and pick the file to bring it all back.

### Dino's

* Dino's, chickens and villagers now avoid lava and fire. They stop at the edge, never walk in, and one that ends up in lava walks out. Water is unchanged.
* Dino's and chickens that do not follow you now walk around freely, up to about 12 blocks from their spot, and come back. In the dino menu Blijf hier is now Vrij rondlopen, and the button Allemaal blijven is now Allemaal vrij.
* Dino's and chickens now stay where they hatch instead of always walking after you. Pick Volg mij in the dino menu to let one follow. The new button Allemaal blijven stops every follower at once. The Dinofluit still calls them, then they stay near you. Animals from older saves are set to Blijf hier once.
* Allemaal blijven is now saved at once, so a quick reload or close no longer makes the dinos follow again.

### Leren

* The typing keyboard now looks like the iPad keyboard. The bottom row has an accent key (é ë), a wide space bar and Klaar. Tap the accent key to pick é, è, ë, ï, ö, ü, ' or -, and you go back to the letters.
* In Examens the parent now picks the lesson first (for example Engels: Runaway), then sees only the tests of that lesson. The question before starting names the lesson too.
* New English lessons from the song Runaway: travel words, asking for your train at the station and telling the time in English. 69 questions and 4 tests.
* New English lessons: Hoe laat is het? Learn to tell the time in English, and why half drie is half past two. 136 questions and 4 tests.
* Toets Precies van het blad bij Runaway: a test that asks only the words and phrases from the school sheet, nothing made up around them. Beautiful People gets it once its sheet is checked against a photo.
* Fixed in Runaway: Excuse me. now means Sorry., just like on the school sheet.

### Leerpauze

* A small grey lock on the lesson, question and teach moment of a learning break. After the pincode a parent can only end the break or go back to it (T55b, AC199).

### Dinowinkel

* Your herd can now grow to 25 dinos instead of 12.
* Full herd, or want a different dino? Tap "Inruilen" next to a dino or chicken in Mijn Dino-verzameling. You get half its price back. Your golden dinos always stay.
* 10 new Bijzondere dino's in the shop, for the real savers: Velociraptor, Pachycephalosaurus, Parasaurolophus, Ankylosaurus, Dilophosaurus, Spinosaurus, T-rex, IJsraptor, Lavarex and the rare Regenboogsaurus (300 points).
* New in the shop: the Dinofluit (60 points, buy it once). Tap 🎶 Fluit in the world and all your dinos and chickens come running to you, nice and slow.
* Fixed: with lots of dinos you could hardly dig or build, because your taps hit a dino. Your dinos now walk behind you and leave the view free. Turn around to see them and tap one to pet it.

### Voor ouders

* New in Ouders: Voortgang. A week report with this week against last week, charts of right on the first try and questions per week, learning and play time per day, every topic with its trend, and the hardest questions. Everything stays on the iPad.

### Onder de motorkap

* The dino shop no longer uses a hidden global dino store. If the world is not ready, tapping Kopen shows "De winkel kan nu even niet kopen. Probeer het zo nog eens!" and no points are spent.
* The tests run Node tests in shared workers and keep jsdom tests isolated, so a test can no longer fail because another test left the dino store behind.

## 0.5.0 (2026-10-24)

### Dorp

* There are 3 villages with little houses in the world. Your old world gets them too, but only in places where you did not build or dig, so your builds stay just the way you made them.
* 8 villagers live in the villages. Each one has its own shirt and hat or hair. They wander around their home and you can walk right past them. You cannot build a block inside a villager.
* Walk up to a villager and a bubble says "Hoi! Wil je een vraag?". Tap it and the villager asks one question from things you have already learned. There are no points, and you can always tap "Laat maar".

* After a question the villagers rest for 3 minutes of play time.

* English: when you fill in a missing word, you now see what the whole sentence means in Dutch first.

### M3: dinos in the world, Dinowinkel, floating in the sky & instant coin practice

* **Stay floating in the sky (`Omhoog ⬆` & `Omlaag ⬇`):** When you fly up with **Omhoog ⬆**, you now **stay floating in the air** when you let go — so you can walk around in the clouds and build sky castles! Use the new **Omlaag ⬇** button whenever you want to float back down to the ground.
* **Override the countdown anytime (`Nu oefenen 🪙`):** Don't want to wait for the countdown clock? Tap **Nu oefenen 🪙** right on the countdown bar (or inside the **Dinowinkel**, **Lesboek**, or **Extra oefenen**) to start exercises and lessons immediately and earn coins!
* **Compelling Dinowinkel (`docs/SHOP_PRD.md`):** Spend your earned coins on four dino eggs: **Compy** (20 🪙), **Triceratops** (40 🪙), **Stegosaurus** (40 🪙), and **Brachiosaurus** (80 🪙)! Each card shows a blocky dino emblem, personality traits, size badge, progress bar toward your next egg, a safe **Kopen → Ja / Nee** confirmation, and your live **Mijn Dino-verzameling** showcase.
* **Hatching eggs & walking dinos in your 3D world:** Bought eggs appear right next to you in the world, wobble, and hatch after 5 seconds of play into baby dinos that grow up and follow you around!
* **Dino menu, naming & knuffels:** Tap any of your dinos in the world to open its menu: choose **Volg mij** or **Blijf hier**, give it a custom name up to 12 letters with **Naam geven** (which floats above its head!), or give it a **❤️ Knuffel** for a burst of hearts and a happy chirp.
* **Full Lesboek & Extra oefenen:** Read any topic lesson in the **Lesboek** and jump straight into practicing that topic, or pick any subject in **Extra oefenen** to earn up to 10 extra practice coins per day.
* **CR1 — 32 building blocks & custom lower-bar chooser (`Alle blokken`):** Choose from **32 block variants** — including stones (`mossteen`, `zandsteen`, `graniet`, `marmer`, `basalt`, `diepsteen`, `vuursteen`), treasures (`goudsteen`, `diamant`), nature (`water`, `ijs`, `sneeuw`), and colors — and tap any of the 8 slots in your lower bar right inside **Alle blokken** to customize your bar!
* **CR2 & CR4 — Obsidian gate floor, fire (`vuur`), stones & molten `lava`:** Right in front of your starting spot lie **4 blocks of `obsidiaan` on the ground** flanked by stones! Build `obsidiaan` up on the left and right to make a gate and place **`vuur`** inside to light the gate, or build with glowing **`lava`**!
* **CR3 — Deploy live chickens (`🐔 Kippenhok`) in the Dinowinkel:** Open the **Dinowinkel** and tap **🐔 Kip loslaten** or **🐔🐔🐔 3 Kippen loslaten** in the **Kippenhok** to release live 3D chickens right into your world!

### M2: learning breaks, sky controls, countdown & parental controls

* **Go higher in the sky!** The sky is now twice as high (`64` blocks tall), and you can hold **Omhoog ⬆** above the jump button to soar higher up into the sky.
* **Smooth camera angle arrows:** Use the **▲ ▼ ◀ ▶** camera arrow buttons on screen (or the arrow keys on a keyboard) to smoothly tilt your view up toward the sky or look around with gentle easing.
* **Countdown to your learning moment:** The top-left of the screen shows **"Leerpauze over 5:00"**. In the last 30 seconds it grows and pulses so you can finish placing your block, and in the last 10 seconds it softly ticks. Opening a menu pauses the countdown.
* **Dino Juf learning breaks:** When the countdown reaches `0:00`, Dino Juf appears with Rekenen and Taal questions! New topics start with a short lesson. A right answer on the first try gives **+2 punten** (and **+3 bonus** if you get every question right on the first try!). If you miss once, you get a second try for **+1 punt**; if it's still tricky, Dino Juf shows a step-by-step explanation so you can learn it for next time.
* **Pause menu & Lesboek:** Tap **Pauze** to see your star points, teleport back to the start spot with **Ik zit vast**, peek at the **Dinowinkel**, or read your **Lesboek** lessons.
* **Parental controls button (`🔒 Ouders`):** Parents can tap **🔒 Ouders** in the top bar or in the Pause menu to set/enter their 4-digit PIN, adjust break intervals and question counts, toggle **Groter lettertype** or sound effects, start or end a learning break right away, and pick Groep 6 / Groep 7 levels and topics.

### M1: the block world

* You can walk around the 3D world. Move with your left thumb and look around with your right. Tapping a button never turns the camera by accident.
* Tap Spring to jump up onto a block. You can't walk out of the world.
* You can mine and build! Tap a block to break it in Hakken, or put a new block next to it in Bouwen. Blocks pop and make a sound.
* Pick your block in the bar at the bottom, or choose one from "Alle blokken". You can reach blocks up to 6 steps away.
* The world has painted blocks: grass, stone, glass, wool and more. Only the sides you can see are drawn, so the game stays fast.
* The world is made from a fixed seed: hills, grass, sand, trees and stone, with an unbreakable oersteen floor.
* Your world is saved: the blocks you built or broke, where you stand, your block bar and Hakken or Bouwen are all back when you open the app again. A save that gets cut off never breaks the old one.
* The app fits the iPad like a real app: no zooming, no text selection, a friendly 'Draai je iPad' message in portrait, and a dino icon for the home screen.
* Behind the scenes: the project builds, has tests, and opens in a test iPad browser.
