# Changelog

Written so kids can read it too.

## Unreleased

* English: when you fill in a missing word, you now see what the whole sentence means in Dutch first.

## 0.4.0 (2026-10-24)

### Punten voor elke ronde

* Every round of oefenen gives points again. There is no limit for the day anymore.
* A mistake can cost a point. You always see how many points you really get or lose.
* Mom or dad can make it easier or harder with Punten in Ouders, Instellingen: Makkelijk, Normaal or Moeilijk.
* New English lessons: the words and sentences from the song Beautiful People. Learn courage, power and how to cheer: Keep going! You can do it!
* Buttons you tap while the game is still loading now say "Even geduld..." and open by themselves when the game is ready.

## 0.3.0 (2026-10-22)

### Eerlijke punten

* The points now add up. What you see is what you get. At the day limit it just says "Goed!".
* A wrong answer never takes points away anymore. A teach moment costs nothing.
* The egg hint is the same everywhere: after a break and after Extra oefenen it follows the Dinowinkel.

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-19)

### The golden egg exam

* A grown up can now start a practice exam from Examens in the parent area. The kid does it on a calm screen with no hints, sees the score, and can tap "Bekijk je fouten" to learn from the mistakes.
* Pass the exam and a golden dino hatches in the world!
* A grown up can stop an exam with the small grey lock on the exam screen. The kid goes back to the world and there is no golden egg.
* If the app closes during an exam, it continues where you were.
* New "🥚 Winkel" button next to Pauze. It opens the Dinowinkel straight from the world.
* Pauze and the Dinowinkel now open right away. The points show "…" for a moment and fill in when they are loaded. You cannot buy anything until the points are known.
* Pauze and Winkel also work when your finger rolls a little while tapping.

### New lessons: leestekens and 't kofschip

* Leestekens: learn where the punt, the komma, the dubbele punt and the puntkomma go, and when you write an apostrof. 5 lessons, 69 questions and 2 tests.
* 't Kofschip: te or de, t or d? 3 lessons with 51 questions about the verleden tijd and the voltooid deelwoord, and 2 tests.

### New lessons: Taal E6

* A new Taal pack for the end of groep 6 with 6 lessons and 105 new questions: tricky sounds (au or ou, ei or ij), long words like vrijheid and garage, plurals like auto's, finding the lidwoord and the persoonsvorm, capitals and ! and ?, and verbs in tt, vt and vd.
* It has 4 practice tests, just like the workbook: deel 1, deel 2, a short M6 test and a big Eindtoets E6.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-19)

The first version! Everything below is what you can already play.

### Chickens and Nu oefenen

* Chickens now cost 5 points each and the coop holds 10 (they do not count toward the 12 dinos). You buy them like an egg, with Ja or Nee.
* When you tap Nu oefenen yourself, your plus points count toward the 10 extra points a day, and you never lose a point. A break that starts by itself still gives all its points.

### Stoppen, a small lock and a grown up check

* In Extra oefenen you can now tap "Stoppen" any time, and you keep the points you already earned.
* The parent lock is now a small grey lock that only shows in the world, and the first time a grown up must answer a sum before choosing a pincode.

### Minecraft look and fit on the iPad

* The questions and lessons now look like a Minecraft menu, with grass, dirt, stone and diamonds behind them.
* The keyboard and the "Ik snap het" button always fit on the screen now.
* The Dinowinkel fits on the iPad screen, with all eggs, the Kippenhok and Sluiten in view.
* The Pauze button works again, buttons are bigger with more space between them, and long lists scroll with your finger.

### M3: dinos in the world, Dinowinkel, floating in the sky & instant coin practice

* **Stay floating in the sky (`Omhoog ⬆` & `Omlaag ⬇`):** When you fly up with **Omhoog ⬆**, you now **stay floating in the air** when you let go — so you can walk around in the clouds and build sky castles! Use the new **Omlaag ⬇** button whenever you want to float back down to the ground.
* **Override the countdown anytime (`Nu oefenen 🪙`):** Don't want to wait for the countdown clock? Tap **Nu oefenen 🪙** right on the countdown bar (or inside the **Dinowinkel**, **Lesboek**, or **Extra oefenen**) to start exercises and lessons immediately and earn coins!
* **Compelling Dinowinkel (`docs/SHOP_PRD.md`):** Spend your earned coins on four dino eggs: **Compy** (20 🪙), **Triceratops** (40 🪙), **Stegosaurus** (40 🪙), and **Brachiosaurus** (80 🪙)! Each card shows a blocky dino emblem, personality traits, size badge, progress bar toward your next egg, a safe **Kopen → Ja / Nee** confirmation, and your live **Mijn Dino-verzameling** showcase.
* **Hatching eggs & walking dinos in your 3D world:** Bought eggs appear right next to you in the world, wobble, and hatch after 5 seconds of play into baby dinos that grow up and follow you around!
* **Dino menu, naming & knuffels:** Tap any of your dinos in the world to open its menu: choose **Volg mij** or **Blijf hier**, give it a custom name up to 12 letters with **Naam geven** (which floats above its head!), or give it a **❤️ Knuffel** for a burst of hearts and a happy chirp.
* **Full Lesboek & Extra oefenen:** Read any topic lesson in the **Lesboek** and jump straight into practicing that topic, or pick any subject in **Extra oefenen** to earn up to 10 extra practice coins per day.
* **CR1 — 32 building blocks & custom lower-bar chooser (`Alle blokken`):** Choose from **32 block variants** — including stones (`mossteen`, `zandsteen`, `graniet`, `marmer`, `basalt`, `diepsteen`, `vuursteen`), treasures (`goudsteen`, `diamant`), nature (`water`, `ijs`, `sneeuw`), and colors — and tap any of the 8 slots in your lower bar right inside **Alle blokken** to customize your bar!
* **CR2 & CR4 — Obsidian gate floor, fire (`vuur`), stones & molten `lava`:** Right in front of your starting spot lie **4 blocks of `obsidiaan` on the ground** flanked by stones! Build `obsidiaan` up on the left and right to make a gate and place **`vuur`** inside to light the gate, or build with glowing **`lava`**!
* **CR3 — Deploy live chickens (`🐔 Kippenhok`) in the Dinowinkel:** Open the **Dinowinkel** and tap **🐔 Kip loslaten** or **🐔🐔🐔 3 Kippen loslaten** in the **Kippenhok** to release live 3D chickens right into your world!

### M2: learning breaks, sky controls, countdown & parental controls

* **Go higher in the sky!** The sky is now twice as high (`64` blocks tall), and you can hold **Omhoog ⬆** above the jump button to soar higher up into the sky.
* **Smooth camera angle arrows:** Use the **▲ ▼ ◀ ▶** camera arrow buttons on screen (or the arrow keys on a keyboard) to smoothly tilt your view up toward the sky or look around with gentle easing.
* **Countdown to your learning moment:** The top-left of the screen shows **"Leerpauze over 5:00"**. In the last 30 seconds it grows and pulses so you can finish placing your block, and in the last 10 seconds it softly ticks. Opening a menu pauses the countdown.
* **Dino Juf learning breaks:** When the countdown reaches `0:00`, Dino Juf appears with Rekenen and Taal questions! New topics start with a short lesson. A right answer on the first try gives **+2 punten** (and **+3 bonus** if you get every question right on the first try!). If you miss once, you get a second try for **+1 punt**; if it's still tricky, Dino Juf shows a step-by-step explanation so you can learn it for next time.
* **Pause menu & Lesboek:** Tap **Pauze** to see your star points, teleport back to the start spot with **Ik zit vast**, peek at the **Dinowinkel**, or read your **Lesboek** lessons.
* **Parental controls button (`🔒 Ouders`):** Parents can tap **🔒 Ouders** in the top bar or in the Pause menu to set/enter their 4-digit PIN, adjust break intervals and question counts, toggle **Groter lettertype** or sound effects, start or end a learning break right away, and pick Groep 6 / Groep 7 levels and topics.

### M1: the block world

* You can walk around the 3D world. Move with your left thumb and look around with your right. Tapping a button never turns the camera by accident.
* Tap Spring to jump up onto a block. You can't walk out of the world.
* You can mine and build! Tap a block to break it in Hakken, or put a new block next to it in Bouwen. Blocks pop and make a sound.
* Pick your block in the bar at the bottom, or choose one from "Alle blokken". You can reach blocks up to 6 steps away.
* The world has painted blocks: grass, stone, glass, wool and more. Only the sides you can see are drawn, so the game stays fast.
* The world is made from a fixed seed: hills, grass, sand, trees and stone, with an unbreakable oersteen floor.
* Your world is saved: the blocks you built or broke, where you stand, your block bar and Hakken or Bouwen are all back when you open the app again. A save that gets cut off never breaks the old one.
* The app fits the iPad like a real app: no zooming, no text selection, a friendly 'Draai je iPad' message in portrait, and a dino icon for the home screen.
* Behind the scenes: the project builds, has tests, and opens in a test iPad browser.
