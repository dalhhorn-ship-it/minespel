# Leestekens: where the content comes from

Source: the founder's request on 2026-10-19 ("punten, komma, dubbele punt, puntkomma, apostrof"). No workbook pages. All questions are newly written.

| Topic | Skills | Question styles |
|---|---|---|
| `punt` | `punt-einde`, `hoofdletter-na-punt`, `punt-of-vraag` | tap where the punt goes, pick the right text, find the missing capital |
| `komma` | `opsomming`, `want-maar`, `ja-nee-naam`, `twee-werkwoorden` | tap where the komma goes, pick the right sentence |
| `dubbele-punt` | `wat-iemand-zegt`, `rijtje`, `uitleg` | tap where the dubbele punt goes, pick the right sentence |
| `puntkomma` | `twee-zinnen`, `kleine-letter` | tap where the puntkomma goes, pick the right sentence, find the capital that should be small |
| `apostrof` | `meervoud-apostrof`, `van-wie`, `letter-weg` | type the plural, pick the right form, find the wrong word |

Overlap: the built in Cito module also has komma, apostrof and leestekens topics, and Taal E6 has hoofdletters en leestekens. This module goes deeper on each mark and adds the dubbele punt and the puntkomma.

Exams: Toets leestekens M6 (20 questions) and Toets leestekens E6 (30 questions, double weight on komma).

The content still needs one check by a teacher or parent (AC124).
