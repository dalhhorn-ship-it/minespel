# Breuken: where the content comes from

Source: the founder's request on 2026-11-09 ("en maak een breuken module"). No workbook pages. All questions are newly written, at the groep 6 level of Dutch schools.

| Topic | Skills | Question styles |
|---|---|---|
| `breuk-herkennen` | `teller-noemer`, `deel-van-geheel`, `breuk-in-woorden` | pick the part, type the fraction from words |
| `breuken-vergelijken` | `zelfde-noemer`, `zelfde-teller`, `een-hele` | pick <, > or =, pick the biggest or smallest, order |
| `gelijkwaardige-breuken` | `gelijkwaardig`, `vereenvoudigen` (E6) | fill the missing number, pick the equal fraction, type the simplest form |
| `breuk-van-hoeveelheid` | `een-deel-van`, `meer-delen-van` (E6) | type the answer of 3/4 van 12 and short stories |
| `optellen-aftrekken` | `gelijknamig-optellen`, `gelijknamig-aftrekken`, `aanvullen-tot-1` (E6) | type the sum, pick the sum |

Only fractions with the same denominator are added or subtracted. Unlike denominators are left out on purpose: that is groep 7.

Typed fractions accept "3/5" and "3 / 5". Where a sum gives a fraction that can be simpler (3/6), both forms count; only the `vereenvoudigen` items ask for the simplest form. The fraction pictures from workbooks (pie charts) are described in words, because the format has no image item.

Exams: Toets breuken M6 (15 questions) and Toets breuken E6 (25 questions, M6 and E6).

The content still needs one check by a teacher or parent (AC124).
