### v22 Duidelijke mol en kompas (0.13.0)

**Status:** next. Asked by the founder on 2026-11-16, in three messages: "Voeg ook een count down toe tot zwarte klodder en een richting pijl/kompas waar hij staat."; a kid bug: "Het is niet echt duidelijk wanneer de mol komt, we hebben door het geel heen gegraven."; and a rule change: "Ok de mol kan niet weg, dat kan alleen met de ridder of een hek. Als je later nog een keer door de gele barrière graaft komt er nog een mol (alleen als de andere mol gevangen is)." Later the same day: "Ok en de ridder kan een mol weg jagen maar dan komt hij stiekem weer terug naar de dieren. De ridder kan maar 3-4 dieren beschermen dus de mol gaat ook naar andere dieren." So a knight chases the mole off for a while and guards a small group; only a pen catches him for good. Built first and shipped on its own as 0.13.0. Part A (the mole) is fun and fixes the bug on its own; part B (the bubble clock and the arrow) builds on it.

**Goal:** "I know when the mole comes and where he is. He stays until I catch him in a pen. My knight can chase him off for a bit and keeps 4 animals safe, but the mole sneaks back to the others. And I can see when the next black bubble comes and where the bubble, Klodder or the mole is."

**Why:** the kids dug through the yellow layer and could not tell what happened. A developer replayed it in WebKit: the mole always came, but the card never said that digging on wakes him, he came up 9 to 21 blocks away while the kid stood at the bottom of a deep hole, queued lines showed late or after he was already out, and some ways out had no line at all. The founder also wants the mole to stay until the kid deals with him, and a clock and an arrow for the bubble.

**Facts checked in the code on 2026-11-16.**
* `src/monsters/bubble-clock.ts`: the clock counts play time only, and only while `canRun` (the bubble switch on, a knight or 30 points, nothing out). At 10 minutes it is due; a break due in under 60 seconds holds it until 30 seconds after the break; with no valid spot it tries again every 5 seconds. Hooks `bubbleClockMs`, `setBubbleClockMs`, `spawnBubble`, `spawnKlodder`, `monsterState`, `moleState`.
* `src/ui/countdown.ts`: the learning break pill shows "⏳" and "Leerpauze over 5:00", pulses in the last 30 seconds and holds the "Nu oefenen" button (`hud-countdown`, `countdown-start-now`). `src/ui/hud.ts` and `src/styles/hud.css`: the top bar holds this pill and the buttons.
* `src/monsters/monster-types.ts`: `LONELY_MS` 60 seconds and `MOLE_TIRED_MS` 10 minutes make the mole leave by himself; `FENCE_MS` 10 seconds with no path makes him leave. The mole is never saved.
* `src/storage/backup.ts`: `BACKUP_FORMAT` is 5.
* `src/knights/knight-ai.ts`: a knight on Pas op de dieren wanders within `GUARD_RADIUS` 7 blocks of his spot. `src/knights/guard-report.ts`: the guard report counts every dino and chicken within 8 blocks of his spot along each axis (its own `GUARD_RADIUS` 8), eggs never, and says "Ik pas op 4 dino's en 2 kippen. Alles veilig!". There is no cap and nothing is guarded: today the report only counts.

**Words used here.** As in the v16 section, plus:
* **Free mole:** a mole who is not gevangen. He is out in the world, or away.
* **Away:** a free mole a knight chased off. He is under the ground, not in the world, for 60 to 90 seconds of play time, and then comes back up. He is still the one free mole.
* **Guard area:** the columns within 8 blocks of a guarding knight's spot along each axis, the same area the guard report counts.
* **Guarded:** a target animal (v16 words: a dino or chicken on Vrij rondlopen that is not on a Dinofluit call; never an egg) that a knight on Pas op de dieren guards. Each such knight guards the 4 target animals in his guard area nearest to his spot. With more than one knight, knights pick in the order of their number, and an animal another knight guards does not count again. So 3 knights guard at most 12. The groups are worked out again at least every 2 seconds. A knight on Blijf hier or Volg mij guards nobody. An animal stays guarded while its knight walks off to poke a monster.
* **Reach:** the columns a mole can get to from where he stands by the walk rule of v16.
* **Gevangen:** a mole whose reach holds at most 36 columns, so a closed wall 2 high around him with room inside of up to 6 x 6 always counts. The check runs with his path search, every 2 seconds, and looks at no more than 37 columns.
* **Target:** the bubble while it floats, Klodder while he is out, or a free mole.

**In, part A: a clear mole.**
1. **The card says what happens.** Mining a Waarschuwsteen shows the card "Onder de gele streep slaapt de Monstermol. Graaf je verder? Dan wordt de Monstermol wakker!" with one OK button. The toast at y 6 or 7 stays as it is.
2. **Lines on time.** Mole lines are shown as toasts on the HUD, so the kid reads them anywhere. A new mole line replaces any mole or warning line still waiting, and shows within 1 second. A waiting line is dropped, never shown after a later one. The rumble shows "Oei... de grond rommelt!". When he is up: "De Monstermol is boven! Hij zoekt je dieren." and then the help line "Bouw een hek van 2 hoog om hem heen.", with or without a knight (a knight does not catch him, part C). "Hihi!" stays as his sound and his speech above him, not as a toast.
3. **He stays.** A free mole never leaves by himself: not after 60 seconds alone and not after 10 minutes. He leaves only when the parent switches him off or with "Wereld opnieuw beginnen". A knight who fills his Moed bar only chases him off for a while (part C). With no target animal he waddles around within 6 blocks of where he came up and says "Niemand om mee te spelen..." once. With every target animal behind a wall he cannot cross, he says "Hmpf! Ik kan er niet bij." at most once a minute and waddles around. He teases at most once per 15 seconds of play time.
4. **Gevangen.** When a mole is gevangen: "Goed gedaan! De Monstermol is gevangen." He sits down with his eyes shut, a soft "Zzz" above him and a small wooden sign "Gevangen!" next to him. He never teases, also not an animal inside his pen, and knights leave him alone. He stays for good. If the kid opens the pen so his reach grows past 36 columns, he says "Hihi, ik ben vrij!" and is a free mole again.
5. **A gevangen mole stays caught.** A tap on a gevangen mole shows a small card "Zzz... de Monstermol slaapt in zijn hok." with OK, with or without a knight. No knight goes to him or pokes him, in any mode, and nothing in the game sends him away. Only the parent switch (In 10) and "Wereld opnieuw beginnen" or Alles wissen remove a mole. A tap on a free mole out in the world shows the help line "Bouw een hek van 2 hoog om hem heen." as a toast, at most once per 5 seconds. Neither tap mines or places a block.
6. **Another mole.** Digging below the stripes wakes a new mole only while there is no free mole (out or away) and no rumble. So the next mole comes only when the earlier one is gevangen. At most 3 gevangen moles and 1 free mole. With a free mole out: "Er loopt al een Monstermol rond." With 3 gevangen moles: "Er slapen al 3 Monstermollen. Meer passen er niet." With a bubble or Klodder out: "De Monstermol slaapt nog even door." Each of these at most once per 10 seconds of play time.
7. **Every way out has a line.** No spot fits: "Hmm... toch maar niet. De Monstermol slaapt verder." No bubble shows during a rumble: the bubble waits and tries again every 5 seconds, as with no valid spot, so a bubble never cancels a mole.
8. **Near the hole when no animal waits.** With no target animal in the world, he comes up on a ground spot 4 to 6 blocks from the dig column along x and z, not within 3 blocks of the kid, with the other rules of v16 In 3. With target animals the v16 rule holds.
9. **Saved.** Free and gevangen moles are saved in the `monsters` record (position and gevangen or free, a free mole at most 5 seconds old; an away mole with his away time left, 0 to 90 seconds) and come back after a reload, a crash or a kill. A rumble is not saved. The backup holds them, and its file format goes one up (6). A backup check refuses more than 4 moles, more than 1 free mole, a mole outside the world, or an away time outside 0 to 90 seconds, with the friendly message of AC195. A save or backup from before 0.13.0 loads with no mole.
10. **Switch and new world.** Monstermol or the master off: every mole digs down at once and none is saved. On again: none comes back until the kid digs. "Wereld opnieuw beginnen" and Alles wissen: no mole.

**In, part B: the bubble clock and the arrow.**
11. **The bubble pill.** A pill of its own on the HUD, under the learning break pill, never in its row and never over "Nu oefenen" or any button. It is round and lilac with a small picture of the black bubble, so it looks nothing like the learning break pill with "⏳". It never pulses, never changes colour and plays no sound, also in its last seconds. It is not a button: a tap on it does nothing.
12. **When the clock runs** (the master, Ridders and Zwarte bubbel on, a knight or 30 points, nothing out): "Bubbel over 3:20", the play time left to 10 minutes, rounded up to whole seconds. Due but held (a break near, after a break, no spot, a rumble): "Bubbel komt zo!". When the clock does not run and no target is out: nothing shows.
13. **The arrow.** While a target is out, the pill shows the target's picture (the bubble, Klodder's face, or the mole's face with glasses), its name and the distance, for example "Klodder: 12 blokken" ("1 blok" for 1), and an arrow. The arrow points along the ground to the target, seen from where the kid looks: up means ahead, down means behind, and left and right. When the target is 3 or more blocks above or below the kid's feet, the pill adds "boven je" or "onder je". With more than one target out, the nearest. Gevangen moles get no arrow. The distance is the straight line from the kid's feet to the target, rounded. The arrow follows at least 5 times a second and turns smoothly; with reduced motion it jumps.
14. **Hidden** in each pause state, while the world HUD is hidden, and when the switch of its target is off (the bubble, Ridders or the master for the bubble and Klodder; Monstermol or the master for the mole). After a pause it comes back with the same text.
15. **Version 0.13.0** and a `CHANGELOG.md` entry in kid words.

**In, part C: knights chase the mole off and guard a small group** (the founder's rule of later on 2026-11-16).
16. **Chased off, not caught.** When a knight fills a free mole's Moed bar (AC251, AC262, AC285), the knight says "Wegwezen!", the mole says "Hmpf!" above him and dives under the ground within 2 seconds, and he is away. The toast "De Monstermol rent weg. Hij komt vast stiekem terug!" shows, then the help line "Bouw een hek van 2 hoog om hem heen.". The knight goes back to his mode. His Moed bar is empty again for next time. A knight never removes a mole.
17. **Away and back.** An away mole stays away for 60 to 90 seconds of play time (picked with the seeded random). The away time stands still in each pause state. While away he teases nobody, has no arrow on the pill (as if not out) and makes no near sound in v24. Then he pops up with a soft rumble on a ground spot by the spot rules of v16 In 3, within 16 blocks of the unguarded target animal nearest to where he dove under, and walks to it. The toast "Hihi! De Monstermol is terug." shows, then the help line again. With no unguarded target animal: he pops up out of reach of every guarding knight if he can, as In 18 says. With no target animal at all: he pops up 4 to 6 blocks from where he dove, and In 3 holds. With no spot that fits: he tries again every 5 seconds and stays away until one fits.
18. **Unguarded first.** A free mole picks only unguarded target animals, the nearest first (AC247 otherwise holds). A guarded animal is never teased by the mole. With every target animal guarded, he teases nobody: he waddles on a spot more than 16 blocks from every knight on Pas op de dieren (more with a banner, AC285) when his reach allows, and says "Hmpf! Overal ridders..." at most once a minute. When an animal is no longer guarded, he goes for it within 3 seconds.
19. **What the kid sees.** While a free mole exists (out or away), each guarded animal has a small blue shield mark floating above it, one plain picture, no animation, at most 12. With no free mole the marks show for 5 seconds after the kid taps a guarding knight. The guard report counts only the animals that knight guards, for example "Ik pas op 3 dino's en 1 kip. Alles veilig!". With more target animals in his guard area than he guards, it adds "Meer dan 4 lukt me niet." before "Alles veilig!". With none: "Hier zijn nog geen dieren." as before.
20. **Klodder is unchanged.** Guard groups do not apply to Klodder. A knight still sends Klodder back into his bubble as AC216 says, and Klodder still tires after 5 minutes.
21. **A fourth knight mode: "Jaag monsters weg"** (internal `chase`), on the founder's ask "Kind kan ook een ridder vragen de mol te volgen en weg te jagen, maak dat een stand." The knight menu shows four mode buttons: Volg mij, Blijf hier, Pas op de dieren and Jaag monsters weg, each at least 56 x 56 points as AC203, and the menu with Uitrusten and Sluiten fits on both iPad sizes, also with Groter lettertype. In this mode the knight walks after a free mole out in the world by the path search, anywhere in the world, at his own speed, and pops next to him after 3 seconds stuck, as AC251. Near him he pokes until the Moed bar is full, and the mole is chased off as In 16. He does the same for Klodder: Klodder goes back into his bubble as AC216. With both out, he goes to the nearer. With no free mole out and no Klodder (also while the mole is away), he walks back to his spot (where he stood when the kid picked the mode) and waits there within 1 block; the mode stays on. He guards no animals. With several knights in this mode, they all go after the same monster and their Kracht adds up as AC262. A gevangen mole is never chased. The mode is saved like the others; a save or backup without it still loads with the knights' old modes, and a backup check accepts it.

**Out:**
* A mole that breaks blocks, follows the kid or hurts anything; more than 4 moles; catching a mole in anything but a wall; a gevangen mole as a pet with a menu or a name.
* A knight that removes a mole for good or sends a gevangen mole away; a knight that guards more than 4; a setting for the group size or the away time; a mole who teases a guarded animal.
* A marker over the target on screen, a map, or an arrow to anything but a target. A clock for the bubble's 60 seconds or for Klodder's 5 minutes.
* A parent switch for the pill alone.

**Kid safety.** The mole now stays until the kid builds a pen. A knight only chases him off for a while and keeps at most 4 animals safe, so the screen must always show the way out: the help line "Bouw een hek van 2 hoog om hem heen." shows each time he comes up, each time he comes back and on a tap on him. Blocks are free and a wall 2 high around him always catches him. A knight is not needed to stop him, and no line says to buy one. No animal is ever hurt or lost; teased animals carry on as AC220 says, and he teases at most once per 15 seconds. The chase line is friendly, never a threat. A gevangen mole is calm and quiet and stays caught. The parent switch removes every mole at once. The pill counts down without pressure: no red, no pulse, no sound, friendly words, and it names no shop and no price. No new personal data, no new text the kid types, no network request.

### v24 Geluid dichtbij (0.15.0)

**Status:** next after v23. Asked by the founder on 2026-11-16: "Kun je ook geluid toevoegen voor de mol, monsters en lava kip truck, dat luider wordt als je dichterbij komt". Shipped on its own as 0.15.0.

**Why:** a founder wish. Sound makes the world feel alive and helps the kid find things together with the v22 arrow.

**Facts checked in the code on 2026-11-16.**
* `src/audio/sound.ts`: every sound is a short recipe with a row in `SOUND_SPECS` (length and loudest gain). `tests/unit/kid-safety.test.ts` checks each recipe: under 1.5 seconds and no louder than the loudest sound of 0.6.0 (thunk, 0.8). The monster sounds peak at 0.2 to 0.3. Volume is 0 to 100 on one master gain, Geluidseffecten uit wins, and no sound plays before the first tap (the audio unlock, AC100).

**Words used here.**
* **Near sound:** a short sound that repeats while its source is out, louder when the kid is closer.
* **Closeness:** 1 at the loudest distance or nearer, 0 at the start distance or further, and in a straight line between.

**In:**
1. **Four sources, each its own silly sound,** made of short recipes under 1.5 seconds that repeat at a fixed beat:

| Source | Sound idea | Name | Repeats every | Peak | Starts at | Loudest at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The bubble, while it floats | a soft bubbling "blub blub" | `blub` | 1.5 s | 0.2 | 16 blocks | 3 blocks |
| Klodder | a giggly hum | `giechel` | 2.5 s | 0.2 | 20 blocks | 3 blocks |
| A free mole | a muffled snuffle | `snuffel` | 2 s | 0.2 | 20 blocks | 3 blocks |
| A gevangen mole | a soft snore | `snurk` | 4 s | 0.1 | 8 blocks | 2 blocks |
| The Lavakip-bar | a soft sizzle, and a little three note tune | `sis`, `deuntje` | 2 s, and the tune every 10 s | 0.12, 0.15 | 12 blocks | 3 blocks |

2. **Louder when closer.** Each sound plays at its peak times its closeness, times the Volume. The distance is the straight line from the kid to the source. The beat never gets faster when the kid comes closer, only louder.
3. **At most 2 at once.** Only the 2 sources with the highest closeness sound; the others are silent.
4. **Left and right.** Each near sound pans softly to the side where the source is, by the same angle as the v22 arrow, at most half way. Ahead or behind sounds in the middle. Safari on the reference iPad has the stereo panner; where it is missing the sound plays in the middle.
5. **Silent** in each pause state, in the learning break, an exam, portrait and the background, before the first tap, with Geluidseffecten uit or Volume 0, and when the source is not there (its parent switch off takes the source away). The bar has no switch of its own; Geluidseffecten uit and Volume silence it.
6. **Version 0.15.0** and a `CHANGELOG.md` entry in kid words.

**Out:**
* Music, sounds for knights, dinos or villagers, real 3D sound with distance filters or echo, a volume setting per source, a switch per source.

**Kid safety.** Silly, not scary: no growl, no heartbeat, no alarm, no siren, no deep long tone, and the beat never speeds up. Every recipe stays under the AC228 caps, so nothing is louder than today. The game stays fully playable with sound off (AC99). No network request.
