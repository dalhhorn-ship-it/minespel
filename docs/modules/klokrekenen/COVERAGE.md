# Klokrekenen: where the content comes from

Source: the founder's photo of a school sheet on 2026-11-09 (a theory box about the 24 hour day, the analog and digital clock, voor en na de middag, a.m. and p.m., plus "Tips voor thuis"). The photo is not in the repo. All questions are newly written; only the skills follow the sheet.

| Sheet part | Topic | Skills | Question styles |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Een dag heeft 24 uur", "de klok gaat twee keer rond", "etmaal" | `dag-en-etmaal` | `etmaal`, `uren-en-minuten` | pick the answer, type a number |
| Analog clock, "6 minuten voor 8", tip "klokkijken in minuten" | `klok-in-woorden` | `wijzers`, `over-en-voor`, `rond-half` | how do you say this time, which time is this, type the time in digits, read the hands |
| 07:54 and 19:54, voor en na de middag, a.m. and p.m., tip "24-uurs klok" | `24-uursklok` | `dagdeel`, `24-naar-woorden`, `woorden-naar-24`, `am-pm` | pick the part of the day, type the 24 hour time, pick the spoken time |
| Tip "bereken hoe lang het duurt om naar school te gaan" | `tijdsduur` | `hoe-lang`, `eindtijd`, `begintijd` | type the minutes, type the end or start time, pick the duration |

Not in this module: the tip about the deeltafels. Rekenen already practises them in the built in `delen` topic.

Clock pictures: the format has no image item, so the analog clock is described in words ("Grote wijzer op de 6. Kleine wijzer tussen de 2 en de 3.").

Typed times accept 7:15, 07:15, 7.15 and 07.15. Typed minutes accept "15" and "15 minuten".

Exams: Toets klokrekenen M6 (16 questions) and Toets klokrekenen E6 (24 questions, M6 and E6).

The content still needs one check by a teacher or parent (AC124).
