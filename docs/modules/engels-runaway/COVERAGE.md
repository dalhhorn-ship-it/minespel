# Engels: Runaway: where the content comes from

Source: a photo of an English school sheet from the founder on 2026-11-15 (Blink Engels groep 5-6 niveau b, song "Runaway", song 4, lesson 2). The sheet has three parts: Words to know, Phrases and Zo zeg je hoe laat het is. No photo is kept in the repo.

The exact sheet text is in `BRON.md`, transcribed from the founder's photo of 2026-11-15 and the single source for every sheet word, phrase and meaning here (decision 127). On the sheet Excuse me. means Sorry.; the earlier longer meaning was made up and is fixed in the lesson, rw-zi-001 and the `blad` topic.

The words and phrases on the sheet are what the kid has to learn, so they appear as they are. Every question sentence around them is newly written.

| Source part | Topic | Skills | Question styles |
|---|---|---|---|
| Words to know (12 words) | `woorden` | `woord-betekenis`, `woord-engels`, `woord-in-zin`, `woord-spellen` | pick the meaning, pick the English word, pick the word that fits a sentence, type the word, find the misspelled word |
| Phrases (7 sentences) | `zinnen` | `zin-betekenis`, `zin-engels`, `zin-gebruiken`, `zin-afmaken` | pick the meaning, pick the English sentence, pick the sentence for a situation, type or pick the missing word, find the wrong word |
| Zo zeg je hoe laat het is (4 clock times) | `klok` | `klok-heel-uur`, `klok-half`, `klok-kwart` | time to English, English to time, half past versus Dutch half, type the missing word, find the wrong word |
| The whole sheet, as it is | `blad` (Precies van het blad) | `blad-woord-betekenis`, `blad-woord-engels`, `blad-woord-schrijven`, `blad-zin-betekenis`, `blad-zin-engels`, `blad-zin-schrijven`, `blad-klok-engels`, `blad-klok-tijd`, `blad-klok-schrijven` | pick the meaning, pick the English word or phrase, type it exactly as on the sheet; clock times both ways and typed |

Levels: M6 asks the meaning, the translation and plain clock times. E6 adds sentences, situations, spelling and the half past trap (half past eight is half negen).

Exams: one per sheet part (Words to know 12, Phrases 8, Zo zeg je hoe laat het is 8), Toets Runaway (20 questions over all parts, words weigh double) and Toets: precies van het blad (23 questions, 23 minutes, only the `blad` topic).

Only sheet text, founder 2026-11-15 ("ja alleen het blad zelf, verzin er nooit iets bij!"): the topic `blad` and its exam Toets: precies van het blad use only what is on the sheet. Every question is a sheet word, phrase or clock time with its sheet meaning, in a short wrapper like "Wat betekent ...?". The answer options are only other words, meanings or times from the same sheet part. No new sentences, situations, names or invented distractors. The lesson is one page that lists the sheet. M6 has the choice questions both ways (46), E6 the typed ones (23). The clock part uses the sheet's four times: 11:00 eleven o'clock, 11:15 a quarter past eleven, 11:30 half past eleven, 11:45 a quarter to twelve.

Typed answers accept a lower case or a capital first letter, and both the straight ' and the curly ’ apostrophe (the iPad keyboard types the curly one).

Overlap: Engels: Beautiful People teaches other words. No other module teaches the clock in English.
